## Blocking is not perfect

- No ad blocker can block 100% of ads, trackers or malicious content. New domains and techniques
  appear constantly, and the bundled lists are a compact, hand-curated starter set.
- Blocking is **not a substitute** for antivirus software, safe browsing habits, or keeping your
  browser and operating system up to date.
- Filters may occasionally block something you wanted (a "false positive"). Use the popup's per-site
  switch or the Allowlist to restore normal behaviour.

## Websites and creators

Many websites and creators rely on advertising. Consider allowlisting sites you want to support.
We are not responsible for any loss of revenue, access, or functionality on third-party websites
that results from using the Extension.

## Experimental features

The YouTube ad-skipping option interacts with a third-party web player that changes without
notice. It may stop working at any time, and its use may conflict with that service's terms. It is
disabled by default and enabled entirely at your own risk.

"Hide annoyances" hides cookie and newsletter dialogs visually. It **does not** accept, reject or
record any consent choice on your behalf, and hiding a banner does not stop a site from
processing data according to its own policies.
